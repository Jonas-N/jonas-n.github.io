Supervision

  1. 2026

    Also his MRes in Psychology (2022).

  2. 2023–2024

    MSc theses at the University of Glasgow, with Rachael Jack: Haoyue Wang (Psychological Science, 2024) and Yueyao Yang (Research Methods of Psychological Science).

  3. 2017–2020

    MSc theses in Evolution of Language and Cognition at the University of Edinburgh: Rayn Epremian (with Matthew Spike), Isabell Seeger (2018, with Simon Kirby), Giorgia Lano (2018, with Marieke Schouwstra) and Tian Li (2017, with Jennifer Culbertson).

I have also supervised 16 student research assistants in Aarhus, Edinburgh, Glasgow and Toruń, working on virtual reality, 3D face capture, multimodal psychophysics and dyadic interaction experiments.

Teaching

  • Guest lectures at Aarhus University (2014), the University at Buffalo (2021), the University of Glasgow (2023) and the University of Edinburgh (2025, 2026).
  • Keynote on virtual reality at the Baltic Summer School of Digital Humanities, Tallinn (2021).
  • Tutor, University of Edinburgh (2016–2019): Introduction to Linguistics and English Language; Origins and Evolution of Language; Introduction to Language Research; Introduction to Cognitive Science.
  • Tutor, Technische Universität Berlin (2011–2013): Introduction to Computational Linguistics; Text Linguistics; Linguistics in Occupation.
  • Writing tutor at the PPLS Writing Centre, Edinburgh, with more than 90 one-to-one sessions on scientific writing.
  • Methods workshops on virtual reality for behavioural research (Glasgow, 2022; Toruń, 2024), open science and reproducibility (Edinburgh, 2019, 2021) and LaTeX (2020).
  • Edinburgh Teaching Award (2020). Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA), since 2020.

Service and organisation

  • Co-founder and co-organiser of MOSAIC, the Multimodal Social Interactions Group, a global research initiative based in Glasgow, since 2021. I built and maintain its website.
  • Co-organiser of the International Society for Gesture Studies conference (ISGS 2025, Nijmegen) and of the ECR Gesture Workshop (online, 2021).
  • Workshop convenor at ICLC-15 (Nishinomiya, 2019), the International Conference on Spatial Cognition (Rome, 2018) and the 51st Annual Meeting of the Societas Linguistica Europaea (Tallinn, 2018).
  • Session chair at EVOLANG (2026), ICLC (2024) and Protolang (2017, 2019, 2021).
  • Co-organiser of the Student Symposium talk series at the Center for Semiotics, Aarhus University (2013–2015).

Peer review. Journals including Cognition, Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B, Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, Topics in Cognitive Science, Humanities and Social Sciences Communications, Journal of Language Evolution, Linguistics Vanguard and PLOS ONE. Programme committees of CogSci (2015–2026), EVOLANG (2018–2026), Intelligent Virtual Agents (2020, 2021, 2025) and Protolang (2025). Grant review for the National Science Centre Poland (OPUS).

Awards and funding

  • Doctoral fellowship of the German National Academic Foundation (Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes), 2016–2019, following its undergraduate (2012) and study-abroad (2013–2015) scholarships.
  • Principal’s Career Development PhD Scholarship, University of Edinburgh (full tuition and stipend, 2016–2019).
  • Co-author of the funded seed-fund project Contextual Factors Shaping the Emergence of Communication Systems, Interacting Minds Centre, Aarhus (2016).
  • Invited participant, selected by position paper, at the UCSB Center for Spatial Studies specialist meeting on universals and variation in spatial referencing (Santa Barbara, 2016), with travel grant.
  • Wiley-Blackwell prize for best presentation (2013) and audience award for best presentation (2014) at the Students’ Conference in Linguistics (StuTS).
  • 18 travel and research-support grants (2014–2020), including from the International Cognitive Linguistics Association and the Max Planck Society.

Public engagement

  • Creating Communication, exhibition at the Edinburgh International Science Festival (2018).
  • Language evolves!, interactive language-evolution activities in Edinburgh primary schools (2019).
  • Popular-science article on spatial cognition, “Woran merkt man, dass man in die falsche Richtung geht?”, ZEIT WISSEN 06/2016.

Skills and languages

Methods. Dyadic interaction experiments and experimental semiotics; multi-user VR/XR (Unity, Unreal); 3D dynamic facial animation and social psychophysics; multimodal annotation (ELAN, Praat); statistics and modelling in R, Python and MATLAB.

Languages. German (native), English (C2); basic Danish, Dutch, Mandarin, Spanish and Korean; Latinum.
