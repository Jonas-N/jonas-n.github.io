Jonas Nölle

I study how communicative signals are shaped by the pressures under which people use them.

I am a cognitive scientist working on language evolution and multimodal interaction. I build interactive experiments, often in shared virtual reality, and use data-driven models of facial behaviour to work out how faces, gestures and words carry meaning between people.

  • Postdoctoral Researcher Centre for Language Evolution Studies, Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń
  • Affiliate Researcher School of Psychology and Neuroscience, University of Glasgow
  • General Editor Language and Cognition, Cambridge University Press
